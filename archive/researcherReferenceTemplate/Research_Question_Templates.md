# Research Question Templates

*A collection of reusable templates for formulating effective research prompts*

---

## Template 1: State-of-the-Art Analysis

```
Research Question — [TECHNOLOGY/FIELD] State-of-the-Art Analysis

Researcher Task:
Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the most recent advancements (from [YEAR1]–[YEAR2]) in [SPECIFIC TECHNOLOGY/FIELD]. Focus on [SPECIFIC ASPECT 1], [SPECIFIC ASPECT 2], and [SPECIFIC ASPECT 3]. Identify key benchmarks, methodological innovations, and performance metrics that demonstrate significant improvements over previous approaches. Highlight real-world applications in [INDUSTRY/DOMAIN], with particular attention to [SPECIFIC USE CASE]. Where available, provide links to open-source implementations, research papers, and technical documentation that would allow for reproduction or adaptation of these advances.
```

## Template 2: Technical Implementation Comparison

```
Research Question — Comparing [APPROACH A] vs [APPROACH B] for [TASK]

Researcher Task:
Compare and contrast the effectiveness of [APPROACH A] versus [APPROACH B] for [SPECIFIC TASK] based on research and implementations from [YEAR1]–[YEAR2]. Analyze differences in [METRIC 1], [METRIC 2], and [METRIC 3], with special attention to performance in [SPECIFIC CONDITION]. Identify the key tradeoffs, implementation complexity, and resource requirements for each approach. Provide concrete examples where each approach has been successfully implemented, including code snippets, configuration details, or architectural patterns where available. Conclude with recommendations for when to use each approach based on project requirements and constraints.
```

## Template 3: Implementation Strategy

```
Research Question — Implementation Strategies for [TECHNOLOGY] in [DOMAIN]

Researcher Task:
Research best practices and implementation strategies (published between [YEAR1] and [YEAR2]) for deploying [TECHNOLOGY] in [DOMAIN] settings. Focus on [SPECIFIC CHALLENGE 1], [SPECIFIC CHALLENGE 2], and approaches to [SPECIFIC GOAL]. Identify frameworks, tools, and architectural patterns that have demonstrated success in [SPECIFIC CONTEXT], particularly those addressing [SPECIFIC REQUIREMENT]. Document scalability considerations, performance optimizations, and integration patterns with [EXISTING SYSTEMS/TECHNOLOGIES]. Where possible, include code examples, deployment configurations, and links to relevant repositories or case studies that illustrate successful implementations.
```

## Template 4: Emerging Trends Analysis

```
Research Question — Emerging Trends in [FIELD] for [APPLICATION]

Researcher Task:
Identify and analyze emerging trends (from [YEAR1]–[YEAR2]) in [FIELD] specifically relevant to [APPLICATION]. Focus on innovations in [AREA 1], [AREA 2], and new approaches to [CHALLENGE]. Evaluate how these trends address long-standing limitations such as [LIMITATION 1] and [LIMITATION 2]. Highlight research groups, companies, and open-source projects driving these innovations, with attention to their methodologies and results. Where available, provide access to demonstration projects, experimental results, or implementation examples that showcase these emerging trends. Synthesize how these developments might reshape [DOMAIN] in the next 1-3 years.
```

## Template 5: Problem-Solution Mapping

```
Research Question — Solutions for [PROBLEM] in [CONTEXT]

Researcher Task:
Map out comprehensive solutions (developed between [YEAR1]–[YEAR2]) for addressing [SPECIFIC PROBLEM] in [CONTEXT]. Categorize approaches based on [DIMENSION 1], [DIMENSION 2], and [DIMENSION 3], noting their relative effectiveness for [SPECIFIC CRITERIA]. Focus on solutions that have been empirically validated or deployed in production environments similar to [TARGET ENVIRONMENT]. Document implementation requirements, potential challenges, and mitigation strategies for each solution approach. Where available, include code implementations, architectural diagrams, or deployment guides that would facilitate adaptation of these solutions. Prioritize identifying modular, scalable approaches that can be incrementally implemented.
```

## How to Use These Templates

1. Replace bracketed text with specific details relevant to your research needs
2. Adjust year ranges to capture the most relevant timeframe
3. Modify metrics, aspects, and areas of focus based on your specific interests
4. Add or remove specific requirements based on your implementation goals
5. Consider adding constraints such as hardware limitations or compatibility requirements

For optimal results, be as specific as possible while maintaining breadth to capture diverse approaches to your research question.
